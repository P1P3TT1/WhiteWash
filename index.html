Load your transparent PNGs and SVGs into the drum. PNGs come out flattened onto fresh white pixels; SVGs get a white sheet tucked behind the artwork so they stay vector. Every cycle runs right here in your browser.

Private by design. Everything runs in your browser – no image is ever uploaded to a server.
How it works. PNG: a white layer is composited under the alpha channel via canvas, then re-encoded as PNG. SVG: <rect width="100%" height="100%" fill="#fff"/> is inserted as the first child, so it renders behind everything.
One caveat. An SVG without a viewBox or explicit width/height can't resolve 100%; those are flagged below and may need fixed dimensions.
